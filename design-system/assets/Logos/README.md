# Logos

The **brain** mark: one bold, continuous `signal-pink` line (stroke 16 on a 120 grid, drawn inside a 0.92 safe area, round caps and joins). It starts at the stem (bottom left), runs the lobes, and folds inward to the `citron` point (radius 10) in a `carbon` ring (radius 13.5).

- **The line is the operation:** all the available data, folded into how the business works.
- **The point is the brains behind the operation:** who decides, the one finding that changes the visit.

Files:
- `brain-mark.svg`: for paper and white (point in a carbon ring).
- `brain-mark-on-carbon.svg`: for dark grounds (no ring needed).
- `app-icon.svg`: carbon tile, corner `radius-icon`.
- `research-button.svg`: the center nav button. Pink disc, carbon brain, citron point.
- `favicon.svg`: 16 px and below. Stroke 18, the point as a carbon dot.

**Lockup:** the mark beside "FieldMind" in Manrope 800, letter-spacing -0.035em, ink. The mark is about 1.3× the cap height.

**Clear space:** the ring's width on every side. **Minimum:** 16 px.

**Don't:** redraw or thin the line, fill the shape, recolor the point pink, add a second dot, rotate it, put it on a gradient, or add literal buildings or doors.
