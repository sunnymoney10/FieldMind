# AnswerCard

One researched answer on the brief's Overview: a short label, a certainty pill, the answer, and the source line.

- Labels: "Who's here", "Who decides", "Energy", "Not sure yet".
- The answer leads with the finding and names the next step when there is one: "Owner: Sam Ratto (2023, may be out of date). Ask who signs for the utility account."
- The source line (`fm-source`, IBM Plex Mono) is the site name and date only.
- Drop the card when there's nothing to say; never show "Unknown" rows just to fill space.
