# Next product milestone: authorized precontact intelligence benchmark

Proposal only. **No real prospect list or real research was used in B2A.** This milestone needs a separately authorized list and an approved research/review environment; it does not require live integration, persistence replacement, deployment or migration into the proof database.

## Small first cohort

Propose twenty manager-authorized businesses with exact list/scope provenance. Include shared addresses/different suites, prior MIMO participation, account ambiguity, owner-operated and corporate/landlord/facilities decision paths. Keep suppressed or ambiguous-scope records out of active research until an authorized manager explicitly resolves the restriction. Do not contact businesses or perform field verification unless separately authorized.

Store authorized working material only in an approved access-controlled environment, never in repository fixtures, examples or benchmark logs. Preserve manager authorization independently from research findings. A manager/reviewer must first confirm the specific list, purpose, participants, allowed sources and storage/retention arrangement. Do not assume that a pasted CSV or an “approved” column grants permission for every activity.

## Required output for each business

1. Who appears to operate this exact location/suite, and what supports that identity?
2. Who likely controls business operations, utility-account, property and HVAC/facilities decisions? Distinguish ownership from these roles.
3. What energy/HVAC context is source-supported, inferred or historical?
4. What is stale, uncertain, missing or contradicted?
5. What should the rep verify first, and why?
6. Does the information change the rep's prioritization judgment? Record before/after and rationale; no automated route or enrollment decision.

Every material answer needs a source/date/scope or an explicit unknown/inference label. Track evidence freshness and access time. Do not force an answer for every field. Unknowns are useful when they prevent the rep from relying on false certainty.

## Independent review, usefulness and speed

Before showing the FieldMind brief, collect the rep's normal precontact judgment/time on a balanced subset. Counterbalance equivalent prospect groups across reps where feasible; avoid showing the same person the answer before measuring the manual baseline. The manual workflow may use the same authorized public sources. Measure total elapsed research plus review time, not just model-generation time; record source/tool wait time separately.

An independent reviewer checks source excerpts and exact premise/occupant attribution without relying on the AI summary as ground truth. Verify authority claims only with appropriately authoritative records or separately authorized representative confirmation. If ground truth is unavailable, label unverifiable and report that denominator separately. A field observation is a different verification method from public-record review.

Report raw counts as well as rates:

| Question | Measurement / proposed pilot gate |
|---|---|
| Identity safety | Zero wrong-suite/wrong-business accepted associations; stop and diagnose any such error |
| Documentary accuracy | At least 90% of checkable material claims faithfully reflect cited sources; report correct/incorrect/unverifiable counts separately |
| Unsupported authority | Zero claims presented as verified authority without matching attestation; unknowns/inferences are not failures merely for being uncertain |
| Provenance | Every material finding links to evidence/date/scope, or explicitly says unknown; 100% of history/inference qualifiers preserved |
| Useful next action | At least 15/20 rep/reviewer assessments identify a specific useful verification step, with reasons and disagreements recorded |
| Prioritization value | Count changed/unchanged judgments and evidence-based rationales; reviewer checks whether change is justified, not whether FieldMind always changes priority |
| Speed | Compare median and range of elapsed end-to-end time for comparable manual and assisted cases; aim for at least 25% less time **without weakening safety/accuracy gates** |

The numeric targets are proposed product gates, not observed results or statistically established productivity claims. Twenty cases are a diagnostic pilot. Report uncertainty and selection bias; do not turn a small, nonrandom convenience cohort into a conversion/production promise. A pilot with many useful unknowns may succeed if it prevents expensive mistaken assumptions.

## Decision after the pilot

Review concrete briefs, errors, rep comments, timestamps and prioritized uncertainties. Decide whether research materially improves precontact decisions before expanding infrastructure or automating more workflow. If the benchmark fails, repair research/source/identity assumptions first. The next milestone is a trustworthy answer about actual research value—not another test-count target.
