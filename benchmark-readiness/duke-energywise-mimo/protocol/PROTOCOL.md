## Study design

Twenty cleared MIMO research cases: five location/identity, five account relationships, five HVAC/energy and five authority/control. Ten matched pairs; two researchers use opposite arms for each case and never see the same case twice. Related site clusters remain in one arm for a given researcher. Case order is randomized with a recorded seed before a real run. Synthetic seed 20261115 rehearses the assignment/order constraint; a future real assignment is frozen before results are seen. Four additional blocked controls sit outside research/timing denominators.

All twenty primary readiness cases are entirely synthetic. Four carry explicit **fictional** manager releases/scope clarifications so the full twenty-case workflow can execute without ignoring restrictions. Their four negative variants retain the original holds. A real case can never borrow these fictional releases. A later release must reference its actual restriction and scope; building restrictions do not silently expand to every suite.

Manual and assisted arms receive the same six-question task, minimal authorized inputs, allowed sources, time cap and output format. Manual uses no generative AI; assisted includes all human preparation, source checking and corrections. Model/tool configuration and source cutoff are fixed in the actual approved environment before the real run.

## Six questions and safeguards

1. Who appears to operate this exact location/suite?
2. Who controls ownership, operations, utility-account, property and HVAC/facilities decisions, independently?
3. What historical participation/equipment and current account/HVAC context are known?
4. What is historical, stale, inferred, unknown or contradicted?
5. What specific verification step should come first, and why?
6. Does a rep's evidence-based priority judgment change from the pre-brief baseline?

All material findings cite original sources and exact scope, with dates/limitations and dependencies. One apparent identity match is never accepted automatically. Public source reports do not become representative verification. Historical devices/account references never become current equipment, account succession or program eligibility. Research output cannot write manager authorization; manager controls are inputs to a separate gate. The benchmark itself makes no enrollment eligibility determination.

## Review and measurement

Research briefs are frozen before review. The study coordinator creates blinded IDs and withholds arm mappings and reviewer keys until initial review/adjudication freezes. Independent reviewers inspect original permitted sources; report source faithfulness separately from independently confirmed current facts and authority. Unknowns, hypotheses and unverifiable assertions never inflate factual accuracy denominators. Critical wrong-business/suite, fabricated-source, unsupported authority or history/current conflation pauses the pilot even if aggregate accuracy is high.

A twenty-minute cap applies to each planned real research run. Include preparation, model/browser waits, verification, correction and brief completion in elapsed time; exclude only logged external pauses. No parallel cases. Pair manual/assisted elapsed times only when both complete with positive times; report timeouts, missing pairs and capped-time sensitivity separately. Record rep usefulness, verification quality, source usefulness and supported priority changes before making a speed/value conclusion. No conversion, routing or enrollment performance claim follows.
