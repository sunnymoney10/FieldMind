# Standard six-question precontact intelligence brief

Use the same brief structure in manual and assisted arms. Blank machine-readable form: `templates/intelligence-brief.blank.json`. No arm receives a more complete or prefilled brief. Program eligibility remains `not_established` unless a separately authorized official determination exists; this benchmark does not make that determination.

Header: opaque record reference, researcher/arm (hidden from reviewer), as-of date, exact location/suite reference, permission receipt, source-policy version and limitations. Display labels distinguish **source-supported**, **representative-verified**, **inferred**, **historical/stale**, **disputed** and **unknown**. A source-supported owner is not automatically verified utility-account authority. Old useful information remains visible with its dates and scope.

## 1. Who appears to operate this exact location?

State the candidate operator, exact premise/suite and supporting source/date. List alternatives and suite/address mismatches. A single apparent match remains a candidate until independently reviewed and explicitly accepted. Keep the listed former business name visible as historical list information, not the current name by default.

## 2. Who likely controls the relevant decisions?

Complete five independent rows: business ownership, business operations, utility-account authority, property authority and HVAC/facilities authority. For each: named role/candidate or unknown, source/verification method, date/scope, state (verified/inferred/disputed/unknown), and what is not established. Public-record/website reports can remain useful source-supported notes under unknown verified authority. Do not infer authority merely from ownership, employment, a brand page, a historical account number or an HVAC permit.

## 3. What is known about energy/HVAC context?

Keep six sections visibly distinct:

1. Historical participation attributed to the previous occupant/account, with original dates or unknown boundaries.
2. Historical Device/Thermostat fields exactly as recorded, including blank/unknown fields.
3. Current occupant identity and its remaining uncertainty (cross-reference Q1).
4. Current utility account/service-location relationship: established by what authorized source, or unknown.
5. Current HVAC/controls: sourced present facts versus building-only records, historical changes and qualified hypotheses.
6. Current EnergyWise Business eligibility: **not established by MIMO history or this research brief**. No inference of current installation, compatibility, service eligibility or participation status.

## 4. What is historical, uncertain, stale or contradicted?

Separate historical facts, age-limited source reports, inferences, unknowns and contradictions. Include competing source excerpts and dependencies; don't average disagreements into a confidence score. Record what was searched but not found without claiming the fact does not exist. Freshness windows are proposed policy versions, not guarantees of truth.

## 5. What should the rep verify first?

Give one ranked verification question/action, the specific uncertainty/risk it resolves and relevant evidence references. Prefer resolving a wrong-suite/operator/account-authority risk before acting on a device hypothesis. This is a precontact research finding, not a live sales script. If clarification would require contacting anyone or private account access, mark that it requires separate authorization; do not perform it.

## 6. Does the information change prioritization?

The rep records the pre-brief judgment before seeing either answer, then the post-assisted judgment and source-based rationale. Use research priority `higher / same / lower / review_hold` relative to the recorded baseline, not CRM pipeline or enrollment stages. “No change, but avoids an unsupported assumption” can be useful. No automatic score, routing recommendation or eligibility decision.

## Minimum finding/provenance record

For every material factual assertion or hypothesis: claim ID/text; subject type and exact scope; origin and epistemic label; historical/current status; observed/published/retrieved dates when known; source ID/URL or historical row reference; exact excerpt/location; inference dependencies/assumptions; contradiction IDs; current-establishment limits; and independent verification method if applicable. Unknowns need a coverage/limitation note, not an invented citation.

Do not populate researcher/source timestamps as if the fixture packet were retrieved public evidence. Synthetic examples are explicitly illustrative; all benchmark result/timing cells remain unfilled.
