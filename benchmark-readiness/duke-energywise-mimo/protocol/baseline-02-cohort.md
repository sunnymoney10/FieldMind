# Balanced twenty-prospect cohort protocol

Future real cohort: twenty manager-cleared **research cases**, drawn only from a separately authorized subset of Brian's approved MIMO list. Do not inspect or screen the real list until the processing/environment gate is released. This document is a prospective protocol, not a real-list transformation.

## Four primary strata, five seats each

| Primary stratum | Five seats should represent | Decision risk being tested |
|---|---|---|
| Location / current identity | Clear suite; shared building; renamed/new operator uncertainty; sparse/conflicting occupancy; single apparent match needing explicit acceptance | Wrong business/suite attachment, history transferred to a new operator |
| Account relationships | Multiple dated references; potentially parallel services; unclear identifier semantics; suite/account mismatch; current account unconfirmed | Assuming succession or treating a premise/list ID as a current account |
| HVAC / energy context | Historical thermostat only; building-level permit; contradictory renovation evidence; shared/landlord HVAC hypothesis; old energy/comfort report | Present installation/compatibility inferred from historical device or unrelated scope |
| Decision authority / control | Owner versus account authority; manager/operator versus decision rights; franchise/corporate; property/facilities split; resolved-control audit history | Collapsing five authority concepts or confusing approval with eligibility |

Assign each candidate one primary stratum before research. Track secondary ambiguity/history flags independently; do not force every flag to be evenly distributed. Include both well-documented and source-poor sites. Do not select only familiar, easy or apparently promising opportunities.

## Selection steps, once separately authorized

1. A permitted manager/data steward confirms the authorized subset, purpose and current suppression scope. Record a snapshot/receipt reference and as-of time in the approved environment; do not copy the full list into the research package.
2. Screen only the authorized metadata and required control facts. Active exact suppression, relevant unresolved hold, unclear authorization or ambiguous suppression scope is an exclusion/hold, **not** permission to conduct enrichment to resolve it. Resolve manager intent through the approved internal process first.
3. Create a ledger of opaque candidates, stratum, known site cluster, review status and exclusion reason. A duplicate address/name is a review candidate, not an automatic merge or accepted premise/occupancy/account relationship.
4. Within each stratum, randomize the cleared candidates using a recorded seed/order. Select five seats and preselect ordered reserves. If fewer than five cleared candidates exist, report the shortfall and request an amended design; do not fill from unauthorized data or change strata silently.
5. Lock twenty cleared cases, criteria, paired assignments, time cap, source-access conditions and analysis rules **before** enrichment. Log every reserve replacement with reason and original stratum; no substitution because a case is hard or yields unknowns.
6. Group related suites/businesses into site clusters for exposure control. Do not infer that members share authorization or identity. Match pairs by stratum/complexity/source density where possible; balance odd stratum counts and record cross-stratum pairs.

The synthetic plan has ten pairs. Researcher A has ten manual/ten assisted cases; B receives the opposite arm for each case. Known related site cases stay in the same arm for a given researcher. Future real cluster assignments may require different pair arrangements; freeze a feasible cluster-aware plan rather than mechanically reuse synthetic IDs. Each real prospect receives both arms from different researchers, not repeat research by the same person.
